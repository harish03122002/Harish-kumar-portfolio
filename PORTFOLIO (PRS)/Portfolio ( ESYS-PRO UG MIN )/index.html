Electronic Shop Management System

Automating manual record-keeping to manage inventory, sales, and billing efficiently.

Context BCA Final Year Project (2023)
Tech Stack Python Tkinter MySQL

01. The Problem

Manual billing was slow and highly prone to errors in stock counting. The electronics retail store struggled to maintain accurate records of inventory, sales, and vendor procurement, leading to inefficiencies.

02. The Solution

Developed a centralized system to track every item from procurement to sale, fully automating the store's core operations.

📦

Inventory Management

Tracked stock levels of electronic items (TVs, Laptops, Mobiles), set low-stock alerts, and implemented category-wise filtering.

🧾

Billing & Invoicing

Built a digital billing module that generates receipts for customers and automatically calculates GST and discounts.

👥

Supplier & Customer Records

Maintained a robust database of vendors for stock procurement and a history of customer purchases for loyalty tracking.
